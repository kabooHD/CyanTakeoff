# Revu 20/21 default keyboard shortcuts (reference)

Facts from Bluebeam's public "Keyboard shortcuts and mouse navigation"
article (Revu 20 and 21), used so CyanTakeoff matches Revu. Tool shortcuts
are pinned by `tests/shortcuts.test.ts`.

Mouse: middle button drag pans, middle double-click re-centres; left click
uses the tool, Space+drag pans, Shift+drag multi-selects; right click opens
the context menu, right-drag multi-selects. Ctrl toggles the wheel between
zoom and pan. Shift constrains lines to 0/45/90°; Shift+click a measurement
caption moves the caption alone; Ctrl+Shift+drag copies a markup in a
straight line; rotation snaps to 15° (Shift for 1°).
